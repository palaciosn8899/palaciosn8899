## About me

I'm a Software Engineering student at **Milwaukee School of Engineering**, building software that connects **AI, full-stack development, and real-world operations**. My projects span construction software, medical imaging research, personalized learning tools, and industrial visual inspection.

At **Edgerton Contractors**, I work on **SmartSites**, contributing to company-wide software with Python, Angular, and C#. My work includes equipment reliability tracking, notifications for mechanics, and invoice automation using Azure OCR.

Most of my work falls into:

- **Workflow automation** — turning manual processes and operational data into useful software.

## Experience & projects

### SmartSites · Edgerton Contractors

Contributing to internal software used across construction operations. My work includes reliability tracking for quad axle trucks and lowboys, inspection and breakdown notifications for mechanics, and OCR-based invoice processing. I've also worked on workflows for converting 2D Trimble Business Center drawings and models into 3D BIM for Revizto.

`Python` · `Angular` · `C#` · `Azure OCR` · `Trimble Business Center` · `Revizto`

### Intracranial Aneurysm Detection · MSOE AI Club

Collaborating on a medical imaging research project using a two-stage pipeline: a **3D U-Net** for segmentation and a scan-level classifier to reduce false positives. My contributions include exploratory data analysis, ROI cropping, on-the-fly augmentation, and research writing. The team trains models on **ROSIE**, MSOE's supercomputer.

`Python` · `PyTorch` · `MONAI` · `SimpleITK` · `NumPy` · `Pandas`

### OptiQual · Xorbix Technologies Innovation Lab

Helped build an **autonomous visual QA assistant** connecting defect detection with decision support. The project combines computer vision, language models, and an AR/VR interface for industrial inspection workflows. Our team earned **3rd place** in the Innovation Lab competition.

`YOLOv11` · `Vision Transformers` · `Databricks` · `Whisper` · `Meta Quest 3` · `Next.js`

### TutorThread / uNotes

Built features for an AI tutoring platform that combines **local LLMs**, document retrieval, and personalized teaching styles. My work includes Ollama integration, a tutoring persona and prompt layer, personalization, and authentication. The application uses uploaded documents to ground responses and supports different ways of explaining course material.

`React` · `JavaScript` · `Node.js` · `Express` · `Ollama` · `RAG`
